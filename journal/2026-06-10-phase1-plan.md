# Phase 1 plan: PPO baseline, locked eval, harness validation

One-line summary: train the 10M-step PPO baseline on DonkeyKong-v5, validate
the harness on Breakout, and pin an operational "survives screen 1"
definition from observed evidence.

## Plan

1. ppo_baseline_10m (64 envs, 12 threads, CleanRL defaults, sticky actions,
   full action set): the Phase 1 deliverable. Throughput measured at about
   5,000 steps per second, so 10M steps takes about 33 minutes.
2. breakout_validation_3m: same pipeline on Breakout-v5. CleanRL PPO reports
   roughly 400 at 10M steps, so 3M steps must show clear learning (well above
   the about 1.2 random-play return). If it does not, the harness, not the
   game, is suspect.
3. Evaluate the DK final checkpoint over 100 protocol episodes, append
   ledger row, plot the learning curve, record a video.
4. Define "survives screen 1" operationally from the video plus the
   evaluation score distribution, then record the definition in ADR 0002.

## Donkey Kong scoring facts (for interpreting returns)

- Jumping a barrel scores 100; hammer smashes score 300 to 800.
- Completing the barrels screen awards the remaining bonus timer, which
  starts at 5,000 and ticks down during the screen.
- So returns in the low hundreds mean barrel-jumping only; a screen-1 clear
  shows up as a jump of several thousand in a single episode.

## Early observations

- At 1.4M steps the baseline's 100-episode mean return is about 315, already
  above the 122 random baseline; the policy is learning barrel jumps.
