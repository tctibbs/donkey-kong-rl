# Phase 3 plan: one-variable ablations against the E11 recipe

One-line summary: eight sequential 10M-step runs isolate each ingredient of
the winning kickstarting recipe (floor level, anneal length, demo set,
reward transform, BC init, plus two extra seeds of the reference), each
evaluated over 100 protocol episodes.

## Reference R

E11 recipe (configs/ppo_bc_twoscreen.yaml, hash 39046445ad30): two-screen
sticky demos, BC init 10 epochs, aux 1.0 annealed to a 0.15 floor over 400
updates, scale transform, sticky 0.25, full actions, 10M steps. Seed 1
measured: eval mean 5,269, clear 0.93 (and 5,076 / 0.90 at eval seed 2000).

## Arms (single delta each)

| arm | delta | question |
|-----|-------|----------|
| R seed 2, R seed 3 | seed only | headline robustness (3-seed mean for the paper) |
| A1 floor 0.0 | no permanent pressure | with favorable EV demos, is the floor still needed? |
| A2 floor 0.05 | weaker floor | floor dose response |
| A3 floor 0.3 | stronger floor | does more pressure hurt score? |
| A5 clip transform | clip instead of scale | does reward magnitude matter once behavior is seeded? (ADR 0003 test) |
| A6 anneal 100 | shorter anneal | how much early pressure is needed? |
| A7 BC epochs 0 | aux only, no init | is the warm-start needed at all? |

The demo-set ablation (single vs two-screen) needs no new run: E10 IS the
reference with single-screen demos (eval 3,289 / 0.70 vs R's 5,269 / 0.93).

Runs execute sequentially from one runner loop (about 4.7 hours total);
each gets a 100-episode evaluation and a ledger row. Decision outputs: the
"what mattered" table for the paper and the final Phase 4 production config.
